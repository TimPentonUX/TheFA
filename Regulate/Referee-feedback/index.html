Prototype directions for clubs rating match officials. Open any one to walk the flow — built for usability testing and stakeholder review.

Version 2 — updated from feedback

What changed in V2

  • Scoring criteria — a side panel on the scoring screen spells out what Poor through Excellent looks like for each area, so clubs aren't guessing at the bar.
  • Less "leading the witness" — once a club acts on an AI-suggested score, the transcript link fades from a prompt into a settled note, so it reads as evidence, not a nudge.
  • Development framing up front — the reports list now explains, in plain terms, why this feedback matters before asking for it.
  • Share with a colleague — clubs can hand a report to coaching staff via a familiar share sheet, straight from the list.
  • A cool-down window — reports open 24 hours after full-time, giving reviewers time to reflect before rating.
  • Mobile: type or talk, your choice — an explicit first choice, with a way to switch method mid-flow, plus a proper mobile reports list and confirmation screen.
